# Marketing Collateral Design & Production

## 1. Purpose
This skill governs the creation, alignment, and production of marketing collateral spanning digital platforms (social media, email, landing pages) and print media. The goal is to produce brand-compliant, funnel-aligned, and technically accurate materials that drive engagement and conversions.

## 2. Use When
- Designing social media graphics, carousels, or banners.
- Creating email marketing templates or newsletters.
- Structuring conversion-focused landing pages.
- Preparing print materials (brochures, sell sheets) for production.
- Aligning content assets with specific stages of the marketing funnel.

## 3. Do Not Use When
- Developing full-scale web applications (use `web-development` skills).
- Writing core brand strategy or positioning (use `brand-strategy`).
- Executing programmatic advertising buys.

## 4. Required Context
- Approved brand guidelines (colors, typography, voice).
- Campaign objectives and target audience.
- Defined funnel stage for the asset (Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Retention).
- Finalized copy and messaging.

## 5. Procedure
1.  **Funnel Alignment**: Determine the asset's stage.
    *   Awareness: Blogs, infographics, social posts.
    *   Consideration: Case studies, product comparisons, webinars.
    *   Conversion: Sell sheets, proposals, landing pages.
    *   Retention: Newsletters, loyalty materials, user guides.
2.  **Format Selection**: Choose the appropriate format and dimensions based on the channel.
3.  **Template Utilization**: Start with established template systems to ensure brand consistency. Utilize brand-locked elements and customizable zones.
4.  **Design Execution**: Apply design principles focusing on hierarchy, legibility, and contrast.
5.  **Technical Production**: Export and package files according to destination requirements (e.g., inline CSS for emails, CMYK/bleed for print).
6.  **DAM Ingestion**: Store final assets in the Digital Asset Management system with proper naming conventions and versioning.

## 6. Decision Rules
- **Social Media Dimensions**: 
  - Instagram: 1080x1080 (Square), 1080x1920 (Story/Reel).
  - Facebook: 1200x630.
  - Twitter/X: 1200x675.
  - LinkedIn: 1200x627.
- **Email Constraints**: Maximum 600px width, use inline CSS, support dark mode, include alt text for all images, ensure primary CTA is above the fold.
- **Landing Page Structure**: Must include above-the-fold hero, social proof, feature benefits, FAQ, and final CTA.
- **Print Requirements**: Must use CMYK color mode, include a minimum 3mm bleed area, ensure images are at least 300 DPI, and export as PDF/X-1a.

## 7. Evidence Required
- Adherence to specific platform dimensions and technical constraints.
- Correct color mode (RGB for digital, CMYK for print).
- Funnel alignment justification in the brief.

## 8. Output Contract
- High-fidelity design files or production-ready exports (PNG/JPG/PDF/HTML).
- Asset metadata for DAM entry.
- Completed checklist verifying technical specs.

## 9. Stop Conditions
- Assets pass technical validation checks (e.g., print-ready PDF constraints).
- Final stakeholder approval is obtained.
- Assets are uploaded and tagged in the DAM.

## 10. Escalation Rules
- Escalate if brand guidelines conflict with platform best practices.
- Escalate if required imagery is missing or below necessary resolution.
- Escalate if requested timelines do not accommodate necessary printing/production SLAs.
